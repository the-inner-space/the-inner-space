About me

01 — WHO I AM
Who is Nayeem Evan?
I'm Nayeem Evan. This website is a personal space where I bring together my interests, thoughts, learning, and creative work. You can learn a little more about me on the About page.
Why is this website personal?
I wanted a place that feels like my own corner of the internet — somewhere to collect the things I care about and let the space grow with me.
What are your interests?
Writing, music, books, ideas, exploring creative things, and learning new skills are among the interests reflected across this website.
What inspires you?
Everyday experiences, thoughts, words, music, stories, and the small details that can make us pause and think.
Are you still learning and exploring?
Yes. This space is a record of an ongoing journey. The Skills & Expertise and My Journey pages may change as I learn, practise, and discover new things.

The website

02 — THIS SPACE
What is The Inner Space?
The Inner Space is my personal website — a digital collection of my journey, personal thoughts, skills, projects, bookshelf, favourite quotes, and more.
Why did you create The Inner Space?
To bring different parts of my world together in one place: the things I write, the things I make, what I learn, and the ideas I want to keep.
What can I explore on this website?
You can explore My Journey, Personal Thoughts, Skills & Expertise, My Projects, Bookshelf, Quotes, About, Portfolio, Contact, and the other sections through the main navigation.
Will the website be updated?
That's the intention. New thoughts, projects, reading notes, and other updates may appear as the website and my interests grow.
Does the website work on mobile?
The pages are designed to adapt to different screen sizes, including phones, tablets, and desktop browsers. The experience may vary slightly by device and browser.

Content & creative work

03 — WHAT YOU'LL FIND
What is the Personal Thoughts section?
It's a place for personal reflections, observations, and pieces of writing. The words shared there are part of my own expression.
What will I find in the Bookshelf?
The Bookshelf is a space for books and reading-related discoveries. The collection can grow over time.

You can explore the work presented on the My Projects and Portfolio pages. Availability and details depend on each individual project.
Can I use or repost your writing?
Please ask for permission before copying, publishing, or reposting personal writing or original work. If you share a link to a page, please keep the original context and credit intact.
Can I use the photographs, artwork, or other media?
Please don't assume that images, artwork, or other media are free to reuse. Check their original source and ask for permission where needed.
Can I suggest an idea or correction?
Yes. Thoughtful suggestions, corrections, and feedback are welcome. Use the Contact page to send a message.
