A SMALL REMINDER

Look closer.
Life is in the details.

Not every beautiful thing announces itself. Sometimes it's the quiet walk home, a familiar voice, a half-written poem or the sky changing colour when nobody is watching.

I want this space to remember those things. The ordinary details that, somehow, become everything.
