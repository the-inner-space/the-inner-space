HOW AN IDEA TAKES SHAPE

From a passing thought to a finished piece.

THE PROCESS
01

Notice

It often begins with something small: a question, a problem, a line I read, or a design that makes me curious. I collect those little sparks before deciding what they could become.

02

Imagine

I explore different directions, sketch the structure and think about how the final experience should feel. The first idea is only a starting point, not a rule.

03

Build

I turn the plan into something real, test the details, make mistakes and keep adjusting. This is where patience and curiosity matter most.

04

Refine

I return to the work with fresh eyes, simplify what feels unnecessary and improve the parts that deserve more care. Then I share what is ready.
