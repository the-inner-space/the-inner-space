Your privacy.
Clearly stated.

This page explains, in plain language, what information may be involved when you visit this personal website, which services help it work, and what choices you have.

Last updated · 21 November 2026
A SIMPLE PROMISE

The Inner Space is a personal, static website. It does not currently offer visitor accounts, a sign-in system, payments, or a built-in message form. This page describes the site as it exists today; if its features change, this policy may change too.

A READER'S GUIDE / 01—12

Privacy should be understandable.

No complicated language. Just a clear explanation of the website, the services it relies on, and the limits of what can be controlled here.

Read the sections that matter to you. If you have a question, use the Contact page when contact details are made available.

01

Who this policy belongs to

This Privacy Policy applies to The Inner Space, a personal website available at the-inner-space.github.io/the-inner-space. It describes the website's current pages and features, not the privacy practices of other websites or services.

02

Information you provide

You can browse the website without creating an account or entering personal details. The site currently has no built-in registration, login, subscription, payment, or contact form.

If you choose to contact the site owner through a third-party service or a contact method published in the future, the information you choose to send may be processed by that service as well as received by the recipient.

03

Technical information and hosting

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to deliver, protect, and maintain the service. This can include information such as IP address, browser or device details, requested pages, and request timestamps, subject to GitHub's own systems and policies.

The site owner does not operate a separate visitor database or have direct access to every hosting log. For details about GitHub's handling of data, review GitHub's Privacy Statement.

04

Cookies and browser storage

The website does not currently set its own advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not use browser storage to build visitor profiles. Your browser or third-party services involved in delivering the site may still use their own technical storage or processes.

You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking some third-party resources may affect how parts of the site look or load.

05

Fonts and external resources

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details. The use of those resources is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

If the website uses other externally hosted resources in the future, this section will be updated where relevant.

06

Links to other websites

Pages may contain links to external websites, platforms, social networks, or services. Opening an external link takes you to a service outside The Inner Space. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices, which this website cannot control.

Please review the policies of any external service before sharing information with it.

07

Analytics, advertising and payments

The website does not currently use a site-owner-operated analytics dashboard, targeted advertising system, online store, or payment processor. If any of these features are introduced, the policy will be revised to explain the relevant data practices.

08

How information is used

Information you voluntarily send may be used to read and respond to your message. Technical information processed by the hosting provider may be used by that provider to deliver, secure, and maintain its hosting service. The site owner does not sell visitor personal information.

09

Sharing and disclosure

The site owner does not rent or sell personal information. Information may be handled by the infrastructure or third-party services needed to deliver the website, under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

10

Data retention and security

The website itself does not maintain a visitor account database. If you contact the site owner using a third-party service, copies of that conversation may remain in that service or the recipient's records. Hosting providers may retain technical logs according to their own retention practices.

Reasonable care is taken in maintaining the website, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

11

Your choices and children's privacy

You can choose not to submit personal information, use browser controls to manage cookies and site data, and avoid opening external links. Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights under applicable law. The appropriate service provider may need to handle requests concerning information it controls.

This website is a general-audience personal portfolio and is not designed to collect personal information from children. No account or child-directed data collection feature is currently provided.

12

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be revised when the website's design, hosting, or features change. The latest revision date will appear near the top of this page. Please revisit this page occasionally if you want to stay informed.

The Contact page currently does not publish a working email address or message form. If public contact details are added, they will be shown there: visit the Contact page.

QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS PAGE?

Clarity matters.

If contact details become available, you can use the Contact page to ask about this policy or the website's privacy practices.
